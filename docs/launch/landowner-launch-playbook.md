# Landowner Launch Playbook

## Before publishing

- Confirm the area boundary is saved and easy to explain.
- Make the rule summary specific:
  access, gear, limits, reporting, and local fee requirements.
- For big game:
  explain `vald` context, quota, and whether co-approval is needed.
- Add at least one strong photo.

## After publishing

- Open the marketing dashboard.
- Pick one campaign preset:
  personal network, Facebook groups, or repeat guests.
- Copy the ready-made caption and tagged share link.
- Download the branded preview image before posting.

## Social posting checklist

- Say where the property or water is.
- Explain what makes the area practical:
  access, species, facilities, or local tips.
- Mention that rules and booking are handled in one place through Heyra.
- Use the tagged link so Heyra can show whether the post drove requests.

## Follow-up

- Watch the promotion signals on the listing and marketing dashboards.
- Respond quickly to booking requests and review feedback.
- Ask satisfied guests to leave a review or practical experience post.
