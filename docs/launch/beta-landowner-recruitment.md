# Beta Landowner Recruitment

## Target profile

- 20 to 30 landowners or rightsholder groups
- Priority regions:
  Innlandet, Trondelag, Troms
- Mix:
  hunting-focused properties, fishing-first waters, and a smaller `vald`-managed sample

## Recruiting channels

- Existing personal networks in hunting and fishing communities
- NJFF local chapters
- Small landowner associations and `vald` contacts
- Existing guides or service providers who can refer hosts

## Outreach message

- Heyra helps landowners publish access more professionally.
- The beta gives hosts a direct hand in shaping booking, rule presentation, and field-use workflows.
- Early hosts get help with listing setup, marketing copy, and boundary guidance.

## Beta checklist

- Verify email and complete profile
- Add property and boundary
- Create listing with clear rules
- Publish or submit for review
- Share listing through the built-in marketing toolkit
- Complete one booking or simulated end-to-end walkthrough

## Success measures

- 20 or more live or review-ready host listings
- At least 5 fishing-ready instant-checkout offers
- At least 10 completed booking or dry-run feedback cycles
