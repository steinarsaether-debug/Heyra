# Press Kit

## Positioning

Heyra is a Norwegian marketplace for hunting access, fishing licences, and trusted outdoor hosts, built around clearer rules, better field use, and stronger landowner tools than a simple licence kiosk.

## Story angles

- Modernising how private landowners publish hunting and fishing access
- Helping guests understand rules, boundaries, and practical conditions before they arrive
- Supporting `vald`-managed and compliance-heavy hunting structures in a more realistic way
- Giving hosts simple social promotion tools instead of forcing them to design their own assets

## Press assets to prepare

- Logo lockup
- Product screenshots:
  listing page, field mode, booking workspace, marketing dashboard
- Founder background and mission paragraph
- Three host quotes and three guest quotes once beta feedback is available

## Media targets

- Jeger & Fisker
- friluftsliv.no
- regional outdoor press in Innlandet, Trondelag, and Troms

## Launch narrative

- Start with beta host recruitment and early guest feedback
- Publish practical case studies from one hunting host and one fishing host
- Reuse approved guest reviews and practical-experience excerpts as social proof
